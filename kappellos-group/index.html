About us

Three brothers. One name for the company.

GND Developers was founded in 1999 by three brothers, George, Nikos and Demetris Kappellos. The company name is their initials. It is still a family business in Aradippou, and the directors today are Nikos Kappellos and Kallistheni Kappellou.

We only build in select locations that are safe and close to Larnaca. The pictures we show are true photos we have taken, or drawings from our architects.

“Our work is based on mutual trust and understanding without any far-fetched promises.”
