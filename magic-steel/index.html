01 / What we do

One trade, done properly.

Steel for concrete. We take the bar schedule, process every piece in the yard and bring it to site ready to tie. Any shape, any diameter the drawing asks for.

Cutting to length
  1. 01

    Cut

    Every bar cut to the schedule, bundled and labelled by element, so the fixers on site never hunt for a piece.

  2. 02

    Bend

    Stirrups, hooks, U bars, radius bends. Bent to the drawing in any shape, without limits on form.

  3. 03

    Cage

    Columns, beams and pile cages assembled in the yard and delivered ready to drop in. Faster pours, cleaner sites.

  4. 04

    Mesh

    Reinforcement mesh for slabs, walls and ground floors, cut to size and placed with the right cover.

  5. 05

    Fix

    Our fixers tie the steel on site, check spacing and cover with your engineer and leave the element ready for concrete.

03 / About

Run by Kostas Andreou, from Mosfiloti.

Magic Steel Ltd was registered in 2019 and is run by its director, Kostas Andreou. The yard sits in Mosfiloti, between Larnaca and Nicosia, which is why the trucks reach Limassol, Famagusta, Larnaca, Nicosia and Paphos in the same working day.

The work is narrow on purpose. Reinforcement steel only: cut, bend, cage, mesh, fix. The same crew that processes the bar in the yard ties it on site, so nothing gets lost between the drawing and the pour.

04 / How it runs

From bar schedule to pour.

  1. 1

    Send the drawings

    Send the structural drawings or the bar schedule on WhatsApp or email. We reply with a price per element or per tonne.

  2. 2

    Process in the yard

    Bars are cut and bent to the schedule. Cages and mesh are assembled and labelled for each column, beam and slab.

  3. 3

    Deliver to site

    Delivery to any district of Cyprus, timed to your pour, so steel does not sit in the way of other trades.

  4. 4

    Fix and hand over

    Our fixers tie the steel, set spacers and cover, and hand the element over to your engineer for inspection.
