What we build

Homes for Nicosia, from the plot to the keys.

  1. 01

    Apartments

    1 to 4 bedroom apartments in buildings of 3 and 4 floors, with lift, covered parking and a storage room for each flat. Aglantzia, Lykavitos, Latsia and Pallouriotissa.

  2. 02

    Houses

    2 to 4 bedroom houses in Geri with covered verandas. The latest ones come with a 5 kW photovoltaic system and energy class A.

  3. 03

    Offices and shops

    Ground floor shops and office space in our residential buildings, built with the same materials and the same care.

  4. 04

    Title deeds and after-sales

    Prompt transfer of title deeds is a priority for us. After handover we stay available, and we can manage the property for owners who live elsewhere.

  5. 05

    Rentals

    Sweet Residence Rentals, started in 2025, lets and looks after apartments in our own buildings.

About

One founder, one city, since 2005.

Sweet Residence is the company of Nektarios Koufettas. He started Akinita Koufettas in Nicosia in 2005 and has been building apartments and houses here since, from Aglantzia and Latsia to Geri and Lykavitos.

The buildings carry numbers rather than slogans. What matters to us is the quality of the build, a clean handover, and the title deed in your name without delay.

Since 2005, true to our values. We build relationships of trust and make sure you are completely satisfied.

How it works

From a first visit to the title deed.

  1. 1

    Visit

    Tell us the area and the size you need. We show you the buildings that fit, finished or under construction.

  2. 2

    Choose

    Pick the floor, the plan and the finishes. You get a clear price and a payment plan in writing.

  3. 3

    Build

    We build with our own team and keep you informed. You can visit the site as the work progresses.

  4. 4

    Keys

    A walk-through together, then the keys. Energy class A on the recent buildings.

  5. 5

    Title deed

    We work to transfer the title deed to your name promptly, and we stay available after you move in.
