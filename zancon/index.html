What we build

We choose the plot first. Then we build.

"We always carefully select the area and plot, and the materials we use are always the best of quality."From the Zancon company profile

Small buildings of 6 to 19 apartments, 1 to 3 bedrooms, in residential streets of Nicosia. Each one carries a Zancon number, from Zancon 1 in Engomi onwards.

You choose the plot, the size and the materials, and design the house with our architect. A pool is optional. Shells meet EU energy efficiency standards.

Offices and shops with flexible interiors set up to the client's needs, like the Alpha Building in Engomi with its roof garden and basement parking.

Our staff help with layout changes to fit your needs, and with securing funding from financial institutions.
