What we do

Specialists in what keeps water out.

Four kinds of work, one team. Open a line to read more.

01Roof waterproofing

Flat concrete roofs and terraces sealed with a continuous coat, worked around water heaters, solar panels, drains and edges. The finish is a bright white layer that reflects the sun.

02Thermal insulation

Insulation boards laid across the roof before it is sealed, so the rooms below hold their temperature in summer and in winter.

03Epoxy resin floors

Industrial epoxy resin floors for workshops, garages and commercial spaces: one continuous surface with no joints, easy to clean.

04Building repairs

The smaller jobs found on the way: cracks, edges, parapets and minor building repairs, done with the same care as the roof.

How a roof is built up

Layer by layer, from slab to sun.

  1. White top coat reflects the sun and takes the weather.
  2. Waterproof membrane one continuous skin, no joints.
  3. Insulation boards keep heat and cold out of the rooms.
  4. Primer bonds the system to clean concrete.
  5. Concrete slab cleaned and repaired first.

A typical build-up. Every roof is checked first and the system is chosen to fit it.
