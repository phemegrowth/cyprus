01 / What we do

One contractor, from drawing to handover.

A project in Nicosia goes better with one firm answering for it. These are the kinds of work we take on.

  1. Houses and small buildings, from the frame up. The structure, the shell and the finishes, run as one project with one point of contact.

  2. Older homes and flats brought up to date: walls opened, services renewed, rooms added. The work is planned around the people who live there.

  3. Drawings, permits, trades and schedule kept in one place, so the owner knows what happens this week and what it costs.
