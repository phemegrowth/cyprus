02Why aluminium

Any colour. Almost no upkeep.

Aluminium profiles come in a wide range of colours, and in every shade of timber for a more traditional look. Try one.

  • Minimum maintenance. Water and a sponge. By the sea, clean a little more often.
  • Insulation. With the right systems and glass, the indoor temperature holds in summer and winter, and the noise stays out.
  • Strength. Its mechanical properties and the way it behaves in our climate make it the ideal material for frames, in place of timber or plastic.

03The factory

A family name on every frame since 1978.

Eirinarchos Voudaskas founded the company in 1978 to process aluminium and steel for houses and buildings. It still works the same way: drawings first, then frames with strength and a clean finish.

We keep renewing the machinery. Production and engraving run on CNC equipment and are checked through specialised software, and the team is trained on new systems as they arrive.

04How we work

From the drawing to the last screw.

  1. 01
