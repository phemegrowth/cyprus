04 / Process

How a project runs.

  2. 01

    Call or WhatsApp.

    Tell us what you have: a plot, a building, a set of drawings or a rough idea. A short call is enough to start.

  3. 02

    Look at it together.

    We meet at the plot or the building and go through what you want, what is possible and what it involves.

  4. 03

    Scope and price.

    You get the scope of the work and the price, so you know where you stand before anything starts.

  5. 04

    Build and hand over.

    The work is built, checked with you and handed over. You keep one number for every question.
