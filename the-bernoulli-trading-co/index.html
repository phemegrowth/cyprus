What we do

The pool, and everything that keeps it running.

  1. 01

    Concrete swimming pools

    Built in place, to the shape and size your garden allows. Structure, finish and equipment from one company.

  2. 02

    Prefab swimming pools

    A prefabricated pool for when you want less work on site.

  3. 03

    Tiles, mosaics and liners

    Ceramic tiles and mosaics, and reinforced PVC liners for a new pool or for relining an old one.

41 years of swimming pools in Cyprus.

The Bernoulli Trading Co. began as a partnership in 1982 and became a limited company in 1985. It has been in the swimming pool business in Nicosia ever since.

About

A Nicosia company, run by Georgios Syrimis.

We work from 7 Pigmalionos Street in the Geri Industrial Area. Georgios Syrimis is the director. The same team that designs and builds your pool also supplies the chemicals, liners, tiles and fittings it needs for years after.

On our earlier website we described the people who do the work as “skilled workers, master craftsmen, technicians and engineers”.

How it runs

4 steps to your pool.

  2. 01

    Free consultation

    We look at the space, how you want to use the pool and what you want to spend.

  3. 02

    Design

    Concrete or prefab, shape and size, tiles or liner, and the equipment to run it.

  4. 03

    Build

    Structure, pipework, finish and equipment, by our own team.

  5. 04

    Hand over

    The pool running, the water balanced with our own chemicals, and you shown how to keep it that way.
