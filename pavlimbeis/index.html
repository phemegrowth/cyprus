Since 1936

It started with a 17 year old in Aradippou.

1936

A business from home

Pavlos V. Pavlimbeis, aged 17, starts trading from his home in Aradippou village: household goods, goat and sheep skins.

1940s

A shop near St. Lazarus

The business moves to a shop in Larnaca near St. Lazarus Church, importing bicycles, furniture, watches and motorbikes. Easy instalments win the trust of customers. A branch opens in Famagusta.

1952

Into land

The firm buys large plots, divides them into building lots for resale and builds a row of houses in Larnaca.

Today

A Larnaca portfolio

Flats and houses, shops, offices, land and building plots, and a hotel. Run from Pavlimbeis House on Stasinou Street.
