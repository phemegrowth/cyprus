What we build

Any form of structural steel construction.

From the first beam to the last bolt, one team draws it, cuts it, welds it and puts it up. These are the structures the workshop makes most.

  1. 01Steel frame housesThe full load bearing frame of a home, fabricated in the workshop and erected on your plot.
  2. 02Factories and industrial buildingsSteel structures for production and storage, from a single span shed to a large hall.
  3. 03Multi storey buildingsSteel skeletons for buildings of several floors, built to the engineer's drawings.
  4. 04Canopies and sheltersSmall and large metal canopies for yards, parking, loading areas and entrances.
  5. 05Metal roofsSteel roof structures for new buildings and for buildings that need a new roof.

About

About

A steel workshop in Paliometocho, run by Dimitris Dimitriou.

Deherco Metal Constructions Ltd was registered in Nicosia in 2004. The workshop is on Arch. Makariou III in Paliometocho, west of the city, and the same team that fabricates a structure is the team that erects it. There is no handover between a factory and a site crew.

In their own words the company does Μεταλλικές Κατασκευές Βαρετού Τύπου, heavy type metal structures: houses, factories, multi storey buildings, small and large canopies, and metal roofs for buildings.

How a structure runs

Four steps from drawing to standing frame.

  1. 1

    Tell us the building

    Send the engineer's drawings, or describe the house, hall, canopy or roof and where the plot is.

  2. 2

    Measurements and a quote

    The structure is checked against the drawings and the site, and you get a written price for fabrication and erection.

  3. 3

    Fabrication in Paliometocho

    Columns, beams and trusses are cut, welded and finished in the workshop, ready to bolt together.

  4. 4

    Erection on your site

    The same team brings the steel to site and puts it up, so the frame is ready for the next trade.
