What we do

Concrete, and the trucks to place it.

  1. I

    Ready-mixed concrete

    Concrete mixed and brought to site in our own mixer trucks, for slabs, foundations, walls and yards.

  2. II

    Concrete pumping

    Truck-mounted boom pumps carry the concrete over walls, roofs and slopes to where the formwork is.

  3. III

    Village and mountain sites

    Hillside plots, narrow streets and forest settings in the mountains of Nicosia and Limassol.
