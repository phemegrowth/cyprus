What we build

A house that starts as a steel frame.

Oikosteel is in the name and in the logo: oikos is the Greek word for house, steel is what holds it up. This is the work, part by part.

  1. 01

    Metal houses

    Homes built on a steel structure instead of a concrete one. This is what we do, and it is on our logo.

  2. 02

    The steel frame

    Columns, beams and roof trusses in steel: the part of the house that carries everything else.

  3. 03

    Roof and walls

    Panels and cladding that close the frame and keep the weather out.

  4. 04

    Other steel work

    Ask about other steel work for your house or plot.

How it runs

From a phone call to a house on your plot.

  1. 01

    You call or message

    Tell us where the plot is, the size of house you want and when you need it.

  2. 02

    We look at the plot

    Access, ground and orientation decide a lot, so we see it before we price it.

  3. 03

    Written quote

    What is included, what is not, and the order the work happens in.

  4. 04

    Frame, roof, walls

    The steel goes up first, then the house is closed and finished inside.

  5. 05

    Handover

    We walk the house with you before you get the keys.

About

A young Nicosia company with one trade.

Oikosteel was registered in 2022 as a business name of PN Kleanthous Ltd, a Nicosia company set up in 2021. We work from Kitiou 10 in Kokkinotrimithia, west of Nicosia, and we do one thing: metal houses.

One trade means the same people, the same materials and the same way of working on every house.
