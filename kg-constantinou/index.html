What we mean by it

Design-led means.

Four things decided before any home is drawn. They are not extras on a price list. They are the reason the home is worth living in.

  1. 01

    Light before finishes.

    Orientation, window sizes and ceiling heights are decided first. The finishes come after, and they come second.

  2. 02

    Plans that fit a family.

    Rooms sized for the way a family uses them: a kitchen you can sit in, bedrooms that take a real wardrobe, storage that is drawn in, not added later.

  3. 03

    Materials that age well.

    Plain, durable surfaces chosen to look better in year 10 than on the day of delivery. [materials and specification to be confirmed with K&G Constantinou]

  4. 04

    One family behind every home.

    You talk to the people who own the company and build the homes. Decisions are taken at the office on Sinopis street, not passed along.
