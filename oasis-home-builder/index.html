What we do

A house that needs little energy starts on paper.

An engineer who designs and builds sees the whole house at once: how it sits in the sun, how it is shaded, how it keeps its heat in winter and its cool in summer.

  1. 01

    Zero energy houses

    Family houses planned to use as little energy as possible: orientation, insulation, shading and solar, decided before the first wall goes up.

  2. 02

    Design

    Plans drawn by a civil engineer with the build in mind, so what is on paper is what gets built, and the cost is known early.

  3. 03

    Build

    New houses in Nicosia and Larnaca, from the foundations to the keys.

  4. 04

    With partners

    Build and design with partners, with one person you call for the whole project.

About

Giannakis Louka, civil engineer.

Oasis Home Builder is run by Giannakis Louka from Latsia, Nicosia. He registered the Oasis name in 2016 and set up G.A.L Oasis Home Builder Ltd in 2019.

He works as a civil engineer who builds and designs, together with partners, and the focus is zero energy houses: homes that cost less to live in, year after year.
