About

A Limassol construction company that develops its own residences.

Exelixis General Constructions builds apartment buildings in Limassol and sells the apartments directly. The same company takes on renovations of homes, offices and heritage buildings.

Builder first, developer second.

Most people buying a new apartment in Cyprus deal with a developer who hires a contractor. At Exelixis the two are one company. The team that prices the build is the team that pours it, and the person who sells you the apartment can walk you through the site.

We are registered in Cyprus as B.K.V. Exelixis General Constructions Ltd and work from our office at Kosma Aitolou 2 in Limassol. Our residences are in Mesa Geitonia, Agios Spyridonas, Trachoni and Kato Polemidia. [one paragraph on the company's history and team size, to be written with the owner]

How we work

Plain terms, visible progress.

  1. 01

    A price that stays the price.

    Starting prices are published for every residence, excluding VAT. What is included is written into the contract, and extras are quoted before they are built.

  2. 02

    A date you can plan around.

    Each residence has a planned handover quarter. You can see the site at any stage and judge the progress for yourself.

  3. 03

    One team after the keys.

    The crew that built the apartment handles the snag list. No handoff between a developer and a contractor.

  4. 04

    Renovations with the same care.

    From a kitchen to a listed building converted into a workspace, finished in 2026. See renovations.
