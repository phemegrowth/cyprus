About

A Nicosia agency with a lemon tree for a mark.

Lemonia Properties works from Office 23 in the Lavinia Building on Mykinon Street, in the centre of Nicosia. It is a licensed estate agent, Licence No. 709, Registration No. 379, and it handles sales and rentals across the city.

Four steps, no surprises.
