What we do

Three kinds of work, one team.

Renotek takes on homes and spaces that need fixing, refreshing or rethinking. Tell us what you have in mind and we will tell you honestly what it takes.

  1. Rooms and homes taken back to the structure where needed, then rebuilt and finished. Planned with you before any wall comes down.

  2. The smaller jobs that never seem to get done. Repairs, fixing and fitting around the house, handled properly.

  3. Making a room work better for how you live in it. A layout, a finish or a corner that has been bothering you for years.

About Renotek

A young Cyprus company, led by its founder.

Renotek Ltd was set up in November 2023 and is run by its director, Nikolaos Viliardos. The office is in Paralimni, and the team takes on work anywhere on the island.

The work covers full renovations, smaller handyman jobs and the improvements in between.

“Renovations, handyman services, and space improvements, serving throughout Cyprus.”
