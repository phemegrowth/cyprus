What we build

A house, from the ground to the keys.

    Finishes and handover

The family

Father and son, and the next company.

Polidefkis Ioannou & Son Ltd was registered in 2006, with Polidefkis (Polys) and Panagiotis Ioannou as directors. In 2021 the family opened PPI Construction Ltd, run by Emili Ioannou. Both companies hold a contractor licence for building works.
