In the shop

Six aisles. One stop before the site.

  1. 01

    Power tools

    Drills, grinders, saws and sanders, with the blades, discs and bits to go with them.

  2. 02

    Hand tools

    Spanners, hammers, levels and measuring tools for the van or the house.

  3. 03

    Screws, bolts and fixings

    Coach screws, bolts, anchors and the small parts a job always runs out of.

  4. 04

    Paints, sprays and rollers

    Spray paints, brushes, rollers and trays for touch ups and full rooms.

  5. 05

    Waterproofing and sealants

    Roof and wall waterproofing, liquid seals and sealants before the rains.

  6. 06

    Workwear and gloves

    Work gloves, jackets and the gear that keeps a crew warm and safe.

About the shop

A local shop that knows its customers.

D&A Top Builder's Materials Co Ltd is a Larnaca company, run by directors Antonis Andreou and Konstantinos Kareklas. The shop sits at Kotza Tepe 33 and serves builders, fixers and home owners across Larnaca.
