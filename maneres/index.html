Development management

What development management means.

You own the land or the capital. We own the process. Five stages, one office, one point of responsibility.

Discuss a plot →
  1. 01

    Feasibility

    What the plot allows, what it costs to build, what it returns. A clear yes or no before anyone draws a line.

  2. 02

    Design team

    We appoint and brief the architect and engineers and hold them to the budget and the programme.

  3. 03

    Permits

    Planning and building permits in Cyprus, handled start to finish. You sign where needed; we do the rest.

  4. 04

    Procurement

    Tendering and contracting the builder and suppliers on terms that protect your money and your dates.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Site supervision, cost control and handover. You get a finished building, the paperwork and the keys.
