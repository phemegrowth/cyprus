Considered design, from the first number to the last key.

Maneres Group is a property developer and development manager in Nicosia, founded in 2020. We build our own residential projects and we run projects for people who own land or capital but not a construction department.

The company

Two things, done the same way.

Developing a building for ourselves and managing one for a client are the same discipline: a feasibility you can trust, a design team held to a budget, permits handled properly, a builder contracted on fair terms, and a handover with the paperwork complete. We do both from one office on Stasinou Avenue.

How we work

Three rules.

  1. 01

    Decide on numbers.

    A project starts with a feasibility, not a render. If the numbers do not work, we say so and stop.

  2. 02

    Design for the long run.

    Plans, materials and details chosen for how the building lives in 20 years, not for how it photographs on day one.

  3. 03

    One point of responsibility.

    Whether you buy a home from us or hand us a plot, you have one office to call and one company answerable for the result.
