01 / What we do

Build it. Sell it. Stand behind it.

A building company and a small developer under one roof. Their words, from their Facebook page: modern, functional apartment buildings, with practicality and trust.

New apartments in the company's own buildings, sold directly by the builder. You deal with the people who built the block, not with a middle layer.

Registered as a building contractor for residential and non-residential buildings. Apartment blocks are the core of the work.

The company started out as building and formwork contractors. The concrete frame is where the experience comes from, and it still shows in how the buildings are put together.

Office and projects in Nicosia. Service area listed as Nicosia, Larnaca, Limassol, Paphos and Famagusta districts.

04 / About

A family company, since 1992.

A.I.K. Kagias is run by the brothers Kyriakos (Lakis) and Andreas Kagias from Athinon 97A in Strovolos. The company was registered in 1992 and before that the family worked as building and formwork contractors. It builds its own blocks and sells the apartments itself.

45 years of experience in construction. Modern, functional apartment buildings with practicality and trust.
