    The property thoroughly cleaned, beds stripped and remade, towels changed, toilet rolls replenished.

  2. Inventory check

    A complete inventory of the contents, with any breakages or repairs reported to you.

  3. Laundry

    All bed linen and towels washed, dried, ironed and returned to the property.

  4. Equipment hire

03 / New rules

Is your holiday let registered?

Under the Hotels and Tourist Accommodations Law of 2019 (N. 9(I)/2020), rental villas and apartments count as tourist accommodation. We can check that your property meets every requirement.
