Our story

Our projects of the 1980s and 1990s set the trend of owner-occupied apartments.

Drag or swipe through the years.

  1. 1977

    Lakis Tofarides founds TOFARCO with one phrase as its philosophy: absolute consistency.

  2. 1978

    The first building, AKAMAS, is completed on Akamantos Street, today Ezekias Papaioannou.

  3. 1988

    Forte Trading Ltd is set up to import specialised building materials.

  4. 1990

    Tofarco Property Investments starts managing property for the group and its customers.

  5. 1991

    The EVAGORAS COMPLEX on Evagorou Avenue, the largest private project in the capital at the time: 10,000 m² of showrooms, offices and homes, and the first multi-storey underground car park, 7,500 m².

  6. 1998

    Tofarco (Hellas) SA takes the company across the border to Greece.

  7. Today

    Energy class A apartments with photovoltaics and electric car charging, such as Chios Anthea Residences.

The group

One family of companies around every building.

From the materials to the parking to the management of the finished property, the group covers the whole life of a building.

  • Tofarco Ltd

    Land development and construction: residential, commercial and industrial projects.

  • Forte Trading Ltd

    Imports insulation for heating and air conditioning piping, polyethylene manholes, inspection pits, septic and water tanks.

  • Tofarco Property Investments

    Management of the real estate owned by the group and its customers.

  • CPC Parking Ltd

    Runs the Evagoras underground car park and builds parking in the centre of Nicosia.

  • Tofarco (Hellas) SA

    The group's company in Greece since 1998.
