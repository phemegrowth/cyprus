01 / Services

One team for the whole room.

From removing the old tiles to fitting the last tap. Bathrooms are the core of the work, and the same team takes on kitchens, floors, ceilings and paint.

  1. 01

    Bathroom renovations

    Old tiles and fittings out, new plumbing, waterproofing, tiling, then the new fixtures. Smart showers, vanities and sanitary ware.

  2. 02

    Waterproofing

    Every wet wall and floor sealed before a single tile goes on, with a guarantee on the waterproofing materials.

  3. 03

    Kitchens

    Kitchen renovations as part of a single room job or a full home.

  4. 04

    Tiling and flooring

    Tile and porcelain on walls and floors, laid level and finished clean.

  5. 05

    Gypsum board, ceilings, plumbing and electrical

    New partitions and ceilings, pipe replacement and electrical work behind the finishes.

  6. 06

    Painting and decorating

    Walls and ceilings painted as the last step, or on their own.

03 / How it runs

What happens behind the tiles.

The parts you never see decide how long a bathroom lasts. This is the order the work runs in.

  1. 01

    Inspection on site

    A free visit to see the room, measure up and talk through what you want.

  2. 02

    Detailed cost estimate

    A detailed breakdown of the work and materials, so you know the cost before anything is removed.

  3. 03

    Strip out and pipes

    Old tiles and fittings removed, plumbing and piping replaced where needed.

  4. 04

    Waterproofing

    The wet areas are fully sealed. The waterproofing materials carry a guarantee of [guarantee length].

  5. 05

    Tiles and fixtures

    Tile and porcelain go on, then the shower, vanity and sanitary ware. One team runs it from start to finish.
