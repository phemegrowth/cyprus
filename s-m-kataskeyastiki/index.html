How external insulation works

Five layers on the outside of your wall.

  1. Your wall Brick or block, cleaned and checked.
  2. Adhesive Bonds the boards to the wall.
  3. Insulation boards The layer that keeps heat out in summer and in during winter.
  4. Mesh and base coat Stops cracks and gives a strong surface.
  5. Finish render The textured coat you see, in the colour you choose.
