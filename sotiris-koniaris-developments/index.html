About

A Nicosia name, on the register since 1988.

The company carries the name of Sotiris Koniaris. He and Theodoula Koniari are its directors. Sotiris Koniaris Constructions Ltd was registered in Cyprus in 1988, and S. Koniaris Development Ltd followed in 1993.

The office is at 6 Dioriou street in Geri, on the edge of Nicosia. If you want to know what we are building, or what we have built, the simplest way is to call or send a message.
