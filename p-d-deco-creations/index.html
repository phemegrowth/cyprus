How it runs

Four steps, in plain order.

  2. 1

    You call or send a message.

    Tell us where the plot is and what you have in mind. Drawings help, but are not needed for a first talk.

  3. 2

    We visit the site.

    We see the land, the access and the neighbours before anyone talks about a price.

  4. 3
