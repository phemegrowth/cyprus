01 / What we build

Building work, on your plot or in your house.

Tell us what you want built or changed. We will come and look, and tell you plainly what it takes.

  1. A new house on your plot, built to the drawings and the permit your architect and engineer prepare.

  2. A new room, an upper floor, a wall opened up. Work on the house you already live in.

  3. Floors, walls, kitchens and bathrooms, for a house or a flat that needs work before you move in.

  4. In December 2025 a sister company, Cyelia Developers Ltd, was registered to build projects of its own. When there is a project to show, it goes here.

04 / How it runs

Four steps, one person to call.

  1. 01

    Call or message

    Tell us where the plot or the house is and what you want done. Photos help.

  2. 02

    Site visit

    We come and see it, and go through the drawings if you have them.

  3. 03

    Written quote

    A price and a timeline on paper, before any work starts.

  4. 04

    Build and hand over

    Work on site, with Christakis as the one person to call while it runs.
