Service

Timber frame houses.

A whole house, built on a timber frame instead of concrete columns and block walls. Same permit, same architect, same finishes. A lighter and faster way to build.

Houseology Construction is a licensed contractor (Contractors Council class Δ, building) specialising in timber frame buildings. We work in Limassol and Larnaca districts.

Why timber frame in Cyprus.

Speed of build.

The frame is cut to drawing and raised in weeks. No curing time between floors, so the roof goes on early and the trades start inside while the outside is finished.

Insulation.

The insulation sits inside the wall, across the full thickness of the frame. Timber itself conducts little heat. The result is a house that holds its temperature in both seasons.

Dry construction.

Apart from the foundation, nothing has to dry. Less water on site, less mess, no waiting, and the inside can be finished as soon as the shell is closed.

The process.

  1. Visit and brief.

    We walk the plot with you and look at access, slope, ground and views.

  2. Design.

    Plans with your architect, or with ours. Structural drawings for the timber frame by the engineer.

  3. Permit.

    We prepare and submit the building permit file and follow it up.

  4. Quote and programme.

    A written, itemised price and a build programme with dates.

  5. Foundation and frame.

    Concrete base, then the timber frame, roof and windows. Weather-tight early.

  6. Finishes and keys.

    Services, render or cladding, floors, kitchen and bathrooms. A final walk-through together.

What is included.

  • Foundation and ground floor slab

  • Membranes, fire-rated boards and treatment

Questions.

How much does a timber frame house cost?

It depends on size, finishes and the plot. We give a written itemised quote after the visit and a first design. [Indicative price per square metre, if the client wants to publish one.]

Can I use my own architect?

Yes. We work from your architect's drawings and add the structural design for the frame. If you have no architect yet, we can bring one.

Does the bank finance a timber frame house?

It is a permitted, permitted-and-inspected house like any other. Ask your bank; we provide the technical file they need.

How long does it last?

Treated timber, kept dry by the membranes and the external finish, lasts as long as the house. Many of the oldest houses in Europe are timber framed.

What about earthquakes?

Timber frames are light and flexible, and the structural design is done to the Eurocodes used in Cyprus. The engineer's calculations are part of the permit file.
