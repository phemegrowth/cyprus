About

A construction company that builds in timber.

Houseology Construction Ltd is a licensed building contractor in Cyprus. We chose timber frame because it builds faster, insulates better and keeps the site dry. We also extend, renovate and build roof gardens.

How we work

Four habits, every job.

Small company, direct contact. The person who quotes the job is the person on site.

  1. A written quote.

    Itemised, with what is and is not included, before any work starts.

  2. A programme with dates.

    You know which week the frame goes up and which week the keys come.

  3. One contact.

    Questions go to one phone number, and they get answered.

  4. A clean site.

    Dry construction makes less mess. We keep it that way until handover.
