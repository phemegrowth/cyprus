What it is

A timber frame house, in plain words.

The structure of the house is a frame of engineered timber studs and panels, made to drawing and assembled on site. Insulation sits inside the frame. The outside gets render, stone or cladding, so a finished timber frame house looks like any other house in the street.

The difference is in how it is built: no wet concrete to cure, so the frame goes up in weeks and the trades follow straight away.

How we build one

  1. The base.

    A concrete slab or strip foundation, like any house. The frame bolts to it.

  2. The frame.

    Walls, floors and roof in timber, cut to the drawings and raised on site.

  3. The insulation.

    Fitted inside the wall thickness, so the walls stay slim and the house stays comfortable.

  4. The finish.

    Render, stone, cladding, windows, kitchen and bathrooms. Ready to move in.

Why timber

Warm in January. Cool in August.

Timber conducts far less heat than concrete or block. With the insulation inside the frame, the house holds its temperature and the air conditioning works less.

How it goes

From plot to keys, in order.

One contractor from the first drawing to the handover. You get one person to call.

  1. Visit and brief.

    We walk the plot or the building with you, look at access and ground, and listen to what you want.

  2. Design and permits.

    Drawings with your architect or ours. We handle the building permit file for the municipality.

  3. Fixed quote.

    A written price with what is included, item by item, and a build programme with dates.

  4. Frame and shell.

    Foundation, then the timber frame, roof and windows. The house is weather-tight early.

  5. Finishes and handover.

    Electrics, plumbing, render, floors, kitchen and bathrooms. A final walk-through, then the keys.

Questions

What people ask us first.

Short answers. For your own plot, call or write and we answer properly.

Is a timber frame house allowed in Cyprus?

Yes. It needs the same building permit as any other house, with structural drawings for the timber frame. We prepare the file with the engineer.

How long does the build take?

Shorter than a concrete build, because there is no waiting for concrete to cure between floors. We give a programme with dates in the quote. [Typical duration to confirm with the client.]

What about fire, termites and damp?

The timber is treated and the wall build-up includes fire-rated boards and a breathable membrane. Details and the brands used go in the specification with your quote.

Does it look like a wooden house?

Only if you want it to. Most of our houses are rendered or clad in stone and look like any other house outside. Timber can be left visible inside, for example on a ceiling.

Can you add a floor to my existing house?

Often yes. A timber floor weighs much less than a concrete one, so many existing structures can carry it. We check the structure first.

Where do you work?

Limassol and Larnaca districts, from our Limassol office and the yard in Alaminos. Ask us about other areas.
