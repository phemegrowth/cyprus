In detail

The four services.

  1. Timber frame houses.

    A complete house on your plot: foundation, timber frame, roof, windows and all finishes. Designed with your architect or with ours. Read the full page.

  2. Timber extensions and roof additions.

    An extra floor on an existing house or a room added at ground level. Timber is light, so the existing structure can often carry it without heavy strengthening.

  3. Roof gardens and outdoor rooms.

    Decks, pergolas, planters, lighting and the structure and waterproofing for a jacuzzi on a flat roof. Done with the building's weight limits checked first.

  4. Renovations.

    Houses and apartments: strip-out, new layouts, electrics, plumbing, kitchens, bathrooms, floors and paint. One quote, one team.
