About

A family business in Paralimni, 1977 to today.

Technotent was founded in 1977 by Costas G. Georgatsos to manufacture, import and trade awnings, outdoor items and awning construction materials. Since 1980 the company works from its own premises on 1st April Avenue in Paralimni, with a showroom, a factory and storage under one roof.

A Nicosia branch opened in 1993. Since 2004 the company also serves the Greek market. Manufacturing and marketing of awnings, canopies, umbrellas, cushions and outdoor items are certified to ISO 9001.

  1. 1977Founded in Paralimni by Costas G. Georgatsos.
  2. 1980Own premises: showroom, factory and storage.
  3. 1993Nicosia branch opens on Stasinou Street.
  4. 2004Office and store in Greece.
How a job runs

From a phone call to the first shaded afternoon.

Open a step to see what happens and what you need to have ready.

Tell us the space: a balcony, a shop front, a pool terrace, a wedding for 200. In Paralimni you can see fabrics and systems in the showroom; in Nicosia at the branch on Stasinou Street.

We measure on site, check sun, wind and fixing points, and propose the system, fabric and motor that fit. You get a written quote with the options.

Awnings and covers are made in our Paralimni factory or ordered from the maker we represent, then fitted by our own team. Rental tents are delivered, set up and taken down by us.

Fabric changes, motor service, new cushions. Spare parts and fabrics for the brands we represent come through us, so an awning from 1995 can still be looked after.
