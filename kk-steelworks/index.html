About

A family steel company, since 1993.

K & K Steelworks started in 1993 as a partnership in Aglantzia, Nicosia. In 1995 it became K & K Steelworks Limited. Today the company works from its own workshop in Athienou Industrial Area, in the Larnaca district.

It is a family company. Charis Keravnos is the director, and the Keravnos family has run it from the start. We make steel structures, sell them and install them, so you deal with the people who cut and weld the steel.

How a job runs

Four steps, one company.

  1. 01

    Call or message

    Tell us what you are building and where. Send drawings if you have them.

  2. 02

    Quote

    We look at the drawings and the site, and give you a quote for the steel.

    The steel is cut, drilled and welded in our Athienou workshop.

  4. 04

    Installation

    We deliver to site and put the structure up.
