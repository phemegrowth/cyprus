03 / About

Central London training. Cyprus since 2008.

Terrain Emirah was founded by Emily Papapetrou, a UK qualified Chartered Surveyor. Before Cyprus she held director positions with CBRE, DTZ and Matthews & Goodman, and spent about 10 years on instructions in Central London, Western and Eastern Europe, India, Kazakhstan and the Balkans.

In Cyprus the firm works for the major banks, the British Ministry of Defence at the British Bases, construction companies and private clients. The office is on Rafael Santi Avenue in Larnaca. The work is across the island.
