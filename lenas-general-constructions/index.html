03 / About

03 / About

A family name in Nicosia since 1983.

Lenas was registered in 1983 as Lenas Aluminium. Over the years the work grew from aluminium into building, and the company became Lenas General Constructions Ltd, a licensed class Δ' building contractor.

We work from our own premises at Polyfimou 9A, in the Strovolos Industrial Area. The company is led by its director, Stelios P. Lena.
