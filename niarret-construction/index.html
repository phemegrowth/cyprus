The year the company was registered in Cyprus.

A family company in Aradippou, building for itself.

Niarret Construction Limited was registered in 2010 and holds a Contractors Council licence for building work since 2012. The director is Loukas Zacharoullas. The company secretary shares the family name: this is a family firm, and you speak with the family when you call.

We build in Larnaca and Nicosia, mostly apartment buildings and houses we then sell or rent ourselves. We stay the owner of some of what we build, so we build it to keep.
