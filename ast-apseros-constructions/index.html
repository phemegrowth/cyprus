About

Three directors, one surname.

A.S.T. Apseros Constructions Limited was registered on 9 June 1986 and has held a Contractors Council licence for building work in Nicosia since 1987. The directors are Andreas, Savvas and Christos Apseros. The office is in Lakatamia, and the projects are in the same part of town: Anthoupoli, Archangelos.

In their own words, from the company's Facebook page: "high quality properties and contracting services".

How it works

From a first call to the keys.

  1. 1

    Talk

    Tell us what you need: a finished home to buy, or a plot you want to build on. One call or a WhatsApp message is enough to start.

  2. 2

    Visit

    See the project, or we come to see the plot. You meet the people who will run the site.

  3. 3

    Agree

    A clear offer in writing: what is included, the timeline, the stages of payment.

  4. 4

    Build and hand over

    Our own team builds it. You get a finished home, with the paperwork done.
