About

A family company, the same name on every job.

Markides Brothers (Constructions) Ltd is family-owned and based in Limassol. It was established in May 1989 and has worked to an ISO 9001 quality system since 2001.

"Our management and crew are dedicated in providing the best possible quality product and service to our clients, while conforming to all pertinent health and safety standards at the workplace."
