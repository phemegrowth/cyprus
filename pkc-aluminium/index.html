What we make and fit

The front door, in aluminium.

The first thing a visitor touches and the last thing you lock at night. We work in aluminium because it stays straight, does not rust and keeps its finish in the Cyprus sun.

  1. 01

    Security entrance doors

    Aluminium entrance doors built to keep a home locked and quiet, for new houses and for replacing an old door.

  2. 02

    Doors with an LED light strip

    A vertical line of light set into the door. It marks the entrance at night and needs no extra lamp outside.

  3. 03

    Minimal black finish

    Flat panels, clean lines and a matt black finish that suits a modern house, an apartment or a renovated village home.

  4. 04

    Other aluminium work

    Need something else in aluminium? Send a photo of the opening and the measurements, and ask.

About

About

One owner, one name on the door.

P.K.C Aluminium Ltd was registered in Cyprus in 2018. Kyriakos Kyriakou runs it as director, from Potamia, a village between Nicosia and Larnaca.

It is a small, owner-run company. When you ask about a door, you deal with the director, not a call centre.

Process

From a photo to a fitted door.

  1. 1

    Send a photo

    A photo of the doorway and rough measurements, by email or the form below.

  2. 2

    Measure and choose

    We agree the size, the finish and whether the door gets an LED strip.

  3. 3

    Written quote

    You get the price in writing before any work starts.

  4. 4

    Made and fitted

    The door is made to the size of the opening, then fitted.
