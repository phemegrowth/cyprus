How it works

From the first call to the keys.

  2. 1

    You call or write.

    Tell us if you are buying, selling or renting, where, and when you want it done.

  3. 2

    We meet.

    At the office in Agia Zoni or at the property. We listen first.

  4. 3

    We agree the plan.

    Price, timing, what we do and what you do. In plain words.
