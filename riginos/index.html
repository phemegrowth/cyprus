The process

How a build runs with us.

Plain steps, in the order they happen. Every build is different, but the shape of it is not.

See a new-build house in detail

  1. We meet on the plot.

    You show us the land and the drawings. We look at access, levels, water and where the materials will go. It costs nothing.

  2. You get a written quote.

    Itemised by stage, so you see what the frame costs and what the finishes cost. You decide what we do and what you source yourself.

  3. We agree a calendar.

    Start date, the main milestones and the payment stages tied to them. Paid for work done, not for promises.

  4. We build, and you can always call.

    Giorgos is on the phone during the day and on site most days. Changes are agreed before they are done, and priced before they are agreed.

  5. Handover and the small things after.

    We walk the house together, fix the snag list, and leave you our number. A house settles; we come back when it does.
