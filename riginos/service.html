How it runs

From the plot to the keys.

  1. Site visit and reading the drawings.

    We walk the plot with you and go through the architect's set: structure, levels, access for machines and where the materials sit. Free, no obligation.

  2. Itemised quote.

    Priced by stage: earthworks, concrete frame, masonry, roof, plaster, floors, bathrooms, kitchen, doors and windows, exterior. You see each number and choose what you source yourself.

  3. Contract and calendar.

    A simple agreement with the stages, the dates and payments tied to work finished. We coordinate with your architect and engineer throughout.

  4. Earthworks and frame.

    Excavation, foundations and the reinforced concrete frame, inspected by your engineer at each pour.

  5. Envelope and services.

    Brickwork, roof, insulation, windows and doors, then electrical and plumbing first fix with the trades we have worked with for years.

  6. Finishes and handover.

    Plaster, floors, tiling, kitchen and bathrooms, paint, exterior. We walk the house with you, close the snag list and hand over the keys.

Questions we get asked

Before you call.

Do I need my own architect?

Yes. In Cyprus the plans and the building permit come from a licensed architect and civil engineer. We build from their drawings and work with them during the build. If you do not have one yet we can point you to people we have worked with.

Can you quote from the drawings alone?

We give a first estimate from the drawings and a firm itemised quote after we have seen the plot. The ground, the access and the levels change the earthworks and the frame.

How are payments made?

In stages tied to finished work, agreed before we start. You never pay ahead of the building.

Do you work outside Paphos?

Our work is in the Paphos district, town and villages. That is where the family lives and where our trades are.

Can I supply my own tiles, kitchen or windows?

Yes. Many clients do. We take those items out of the quote and tell you the dates they need to be on site.

What happens after handover?

We walk the house with you, fix the snag list, and leave you our number. If something settles or needs adjusting in the first months, you call Giorgos.
