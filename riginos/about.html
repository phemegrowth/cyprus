How we work

Three things we do not bend on.

01

The quote is itemised.

Every stage has its own number. You can take things out, add things in, and see exactly what changed.

02

Paid for work done.

Payments follow finished stages, agreed in writing before the first dig.

03

One phone number.

You talk to the person who is building your house, not to an office. Changes are agreed with him, on site, before they happen.

Where

Konia and the villages around it.

We are based in Konia, just above Paphos town. Most of our work is within a short drive: the town itself, the villages on the hills behind it and the coast either side.
