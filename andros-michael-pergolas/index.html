How it runs

From a photo of the space to a finished roof.

    Send a photo

    WhatsApp a picture of the roof, veranda or garden and roughly what you have in mind.

  2. 2

    He comes to measure

    A visit to see the space, take measurements and talk through timber, finish and colour.

  3. 3

    You get the price and the timing before any work starts.

  4. 4

    The timber is cut, built and fitted on site.
