02 Services

Three things we build.

A

False ceilings

Suspended plasterboard ceilings on a metal frame. They hide pipes, ducts and cables, take recessed lights and air conditioning, and give a room a clean, level finish.

Ask for a ceiling price
B

Partition walls

New walls in metal stud and board to split a room, make an office, add a wardrobe wall or close off a space. Quick to build, with no heavy masonry.

Renovations

From stripping out an old room to handing it back finished. Ceilings, walls and the finishing around them, with one person to call.

Measure, frame, board, finish.

  1. 1

    Visit and measure
