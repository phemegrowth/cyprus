01 / Steel houses

What we build

A house that stands on steel.

The company is named for one kind of work: houses built on a steel frame. Below is what that covers, in plain words.

  1. 01

    Steel houses

    Homes built on a steel frame instead of a concrete one. The frame is set out, raised and fixed on your plot.

  2. 02

    Steel structures

    Frames in steel for a house, an addition or an outbuilding, measured to the drawings and the site.

  3. 03

    One person to talk to

    C.K. are the owner's initials. From the first call to the finished frame you deal with Christos Kokkinos.

About

Owner-run, from Xylofagou.

2018 Registered as C.K. Steel House Constructions

C.K. Steel House Constructions is a business owned and run by Christos Kokkinos. It was registered in September 2018 and works out of Xylofagou, a village on the east side of the Larnaca district.

It is a small business by design. The person who quotes your house is the person responsible for it, and you can reach him directly on his mobile.

How it runs

From the first call to the frame.

  1. 01

    You call or send a WhatsApp

    Tell us where the plot is and what you want to build. A photo or a drawing helps.

  2. 02

    We look at the site

    Access, ground and size decide how the frame is set out, so we see it first.

  3. 03

    You get a written quote

    What is included, what is not, and the order the work runs in.

  4. 04

    The frame goes up

    Steel is set out, raised and fixed, and you know who to call at every stage.
