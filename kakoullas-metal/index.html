01 / What we make

Three kinds of steel work, from one workshop.

We cut, shape and weld steel in Ypsonas, then bring it to your site and fit it. Tell us what you need built and where.

  1. 01

    Steel structures for buildings

    Steel frames and structures for buildings and facilities, made in the workshop and installed on site.

  2. 02

    Fences

    Metal fencing for homes, plots and work yards, made to the length and height of your boundary.

  3. 03

    Metal processing

    Cutting, shaping and welding of steel parts to your drawing or to measure.

03 / The workshop

A metal workshop in Ypsonas.

04 / How it runs

From your call to steel on site.

  2. 01

    Call or WhatsApp.

    Send a photo, a drawing or a few measurements. We tell you how it can be made.

  3. 02

    Measure.

    We take the exact measurements, then agree the steel, the finish and the price with you.

  4. 03

    Make.

    The parts are cut, welded and prepared in the workshop in Ypsonas.

  5. 04

    Install.

    We bring it to your site in Limassol and fit it in place.
