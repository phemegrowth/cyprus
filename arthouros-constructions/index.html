01 / Services

Three parts of a home, one company.

The work is walls, roofs and floors. Tell us which one, where the property is in Limassol, and what you want it to look like when it is finished.

  1. Cracked, damp or tired walls, inside or outside. Send a photo of the wall on WhatsApp and say how you want it to look.

  2. A leak after the first rain, worn tiles, or a roof that needs redoing. Tell us what you see from inside and from the street.

  3. Old tiles, uneven floors, or a new finish for one room or a whole flat. Give us the size of the room and a photo, and we take it from there.
