Method

How we run a site.

The same four steps on a hospital wing and on a house. The size changes, the order does not.

  1. 01

    Price from the drawings.

    We take your architect's and engineer's drawings and return a priced bill of quantities, not a lump sum with surprises. You see what each part of the building costs.

  2. 02

    Programme before the first pour.

    A written programme with the milestones that matter to you: frame, roof, closed shell, handover. Dates you can plan around.

  3. 03

    One site manager, one phone number.

    A named person on site every day who answers your calls. Decisions get logged, not lost on a WhatsApp group.

  4. 04

    Handover with the paperwork.

    Certificates, as-built notes and the snag list closed before we leave. The building is yours to use the day we hand it over.
