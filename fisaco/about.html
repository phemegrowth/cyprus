About

A contractor from Frenaros, licensed for the biggest jobs in Cyprus.

Fisaco Ltd has been building since 2005. The company is registered in Frenaros, Famagusta district, and holds a class A licence from the Cyprus Contractors Council for building works.

The company

Two decades on site.

The work is building contracting: taking an architect's and engineer's drawings and delivering the finished building. Houses, residential projects, extensions, and healthcare and public buildings.

Licence

Class A, Cyprus Contractors Council.

The Council licenses every building contractor in Cyprus and grades them by class. Class A is the highest class, with no upper limit on the value of the building contracts the company may sign. It is the class demanded for hospitals, schools and large residential blocks.
