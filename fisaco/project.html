The project.

Fisaco Ltd is the building contractor for the extension of LITO Private Hospital. Works started in February 2026. The hospital remains in use, so the site is run in phases with separate access for construction traffic and controls on dust, noise and vibration near the wards.

What class A means here.

A hospital extension sits at the top end of building contracts in Cyprus. Our Cyprus Contractors Council class A licence covers it without a cap on contract size. It also means a site manager on site daily, a written programme and insurance to match.
