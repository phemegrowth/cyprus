How it is made

What you can count on.

Same finish as the frames
Railings and shade structures are coated in the colour of the windows and doors.
Measured on the slab
We measure the finished balcony or terrace, so posts land where they should.
Made and fitted
Made in the Lakatamia workshop and fitted by the same team.

Questions

Asked before the first visit.

  1. Glass keeps the view. Bars give more privacy and shade. The frame builder draws both on your length so you can compare.

  2. Send a photo of the frames and tell us the colour if you know it. We will tell you how close we can get.

  3. The width, the depth and the height, and a photo of where it will stand.

Have the sizes. Get the price in writing.
