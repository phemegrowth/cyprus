Aluminium facades.

Aluminium frames and glazing for the front of a building: shop fronts, entrances, stairwells and whole elevations. Made to the drawings in our workshop in Lakatamia and fitted on site, opening by opening.

How it is made

What you can count on.

From your drawings
We work from the architect's elevations, or measure the building ourselves when there are none.
Made in Lakatamia
Profiles are cut and assembled in the workshop, then brought to site ready to fit.
Fitted in order
Opening by opening, checked with you as each part of the building closes.
One finish
Powder coated in a RAL colour or anodised, the same across the whole elevation.
