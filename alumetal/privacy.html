Privacy.

What this website does with your details, in plain words.

01Who we are

This website belongs to Alumetal Limited (HE 3226), Ergatikotitas 21, Lakatamia Industrial Area, 2326 Nicosia, Cyprus. For anything about your details, write to info@alumetal.com.cy or call +357 22 329947.

02What we collect

Only what you write in the enquiry form: your name, phone, email, the kind of job and your message, including a frame list if you made one. We do not ask for anything else.

03Why

To reply to your enquiry and, if you go ahead, to prepare the written price and the job. We do not use your details for advertising and we do not sell or pass them to anyone else.

04How long

We keep an enquiry for as long as the conversation or the job lasts, and afterwards for as long as the law asks us to keep business records.

05The frame builder

The list you make in the frame builder is kept in your own browser, on your own device. It reaches us only if you send it. “Clear the list” removes it.

06Other services

The letters on this site are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google sees the address of your device when a page opens. The map on the contact page loads from Google Maps only after you press “Show the map here”. A WhatsApp link opens WhatsApp, which has its own terms.

07Your rights

You can ask to see the details we hold about you, to correct them or to have them deleted. Write to us and we will answer within one month. You can also complain to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus.
