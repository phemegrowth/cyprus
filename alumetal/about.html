About

On the register since 1970.

Alumetal Limited is an aluminium workshop in the Lakatamia Industrial Area of Nicosia, and one of the older companies on the Cyprus register.

1970

The company

A Nicosia workshop with a long name on the register.

The company number, HE 3226, is one of the low ones: it was given in September 1970. The directors are Christos Galazis and Charalampos Galazis.

The work is aluminium: facades for buildings and constructions made to order. The frames are made in the workshop and fitted on site.

How it runs

From the first call to the last frame fitted.

  2. 01

    Call or send the drawings

    Phone the workshop or send plans and photos by email. Tell us the building, the openings and when you need it.

  3. 02

    Measure on site

    We come and take the exact sizes, so the frames fit the walls you have and not the ones on paper.

  4. 03

    Price in writing

    You get the systems, the finishes and the price written down before anything is cut.

  5. 04

    Make and fit

    The frames are made in Lakatamia and fitted by the same team, then checked with you on site.
