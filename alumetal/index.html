01 / What we make

Aluminium, cut and fitted for the building in front of us.

Every opening and every facade has its own sizes. We measure, make the frames in our workshop and fit them on site.

  1. Facade works: aluminium frames and glazing made to the drawings and fitted on site, opening by opening.

  2. Frames and structures made in the workshop in Lakatamia, then brought to site ready to fit. Tell us the job and we will tell you if aluminium is the right material for it.

  3. Sliding, folding and hinged systems for homes and new builds, measured on site and made to size.

  4. Balcony railings, stair rails and shade structures in aluminium, finished to match the frames on the same building.

03 / About

A Nicosia workshop with a long name on the register.

Alumetal Limited is one of the older companies on the Cyprus register, with a company number from 1970. The directors are Christos Galazis and Charalampos Galazis.

The workshop is at Ergatikotitas 21 in the Lakatamia Industrial Area, a short drive from the centre of Nicosia. That is where the frames are made, and where you can come and talk through a job.

04 / How it runs

From the first call to the last frame fitted.

  2. 01

    Call or send the drawings

    Phone the workshop or send plans and photos by email. Tell us the building, the openings and when you need it.

  3. 02

    Measure on site

    We come and take the exact sizes, so the frames fit the walls you have and not the ones on paper.

  4. 03

    Price in writing

    You get the systems, the finishes and the price written down before anything is cut.

  5. 04

    Make and fit

    The frames are made in Lakatamia and fitted by the same team, then checked with you on site.
