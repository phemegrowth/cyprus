How it is made

What you can count on.

Measured on site
We take the sizes from the walls you have, not from the plan.
Made to size
Each frame is cut for its own opening in the Lakatamia workshop.
Glass by room
Clear, tinted or frosted, chosen room by room.
Colour
Powder coated in a RAL colour or anodised. See each one on your sizes in the frame builder.

Questions

Asked before the first visit.

  2. Anthracite, black, white and anodised silver are the ones people ask about most. The frame builder shows each one on your own sizes.

  3. Yes. The frames are made in Lakatamia and fitted by the same team.
