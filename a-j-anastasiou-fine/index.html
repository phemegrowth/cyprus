About

A family business in Yeroskipou.

A.J. Anastasiou Fine Homes Ltd is a family run property company based in the Paphos area. Our first website went online in 2006. Since then we have helped buyers, owners and tenants, from people relocating to Cyprus to owners of a second home.

We have first hand experience of buying, building and renting in Cyprus, so we understand the money, the paperwork and the worry that come with it. We take it one step at a time, with no pressure.
