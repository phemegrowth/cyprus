What we build

A house in a short time, at a fair price.

Two ways to get there: a house we import, or one we build here in Cyprus with sandwich-panel walls. Both come in the size and layout you need.

  1. 01

    Imported prefabricated houses.

    Our own imports of prefabricated homes, chosen for Cyprus and placed on your plot.

  2. 02

    Built in Cyprus.

    Metal houses made locally with sandwich-panel walls. Light, insulated and quick to put up.

  3. 03

    Your size and layout.

    1, 2 or 3 bedrooms, with 1 or 2 bathrooms. The layout is set to how you will live in it.

  4. 04

    Your colours.

    Pick the exterior and interior finish from our colour options before the build starts.

About

A small team in Larnaca, since 2023.

G.P.P Geo Homes has worked in prefabricated houses in Cyprus since 2023. We import homes ourselves and we build them here, so you can choose what fits your plot and your budget.
