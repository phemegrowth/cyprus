What we tile

Rooms where the tiling is the design.

Walls and floors from the first level line to the last silicone joint. Showers, niches, vanity walls and tiled baths, with edges mitred instead of trimmed.

Splashbacks, feature walls and kitchen floors, set out so the joints line up with the cabinets and the worktop.

Large marble and marble effect slabs, handled, cut and matched so the veins run on from one piece to the next.

Floor tiling for living rooms, terraces and whole apartments, flat and level from wall to wall.

Cracked or loose tiles, or damage you do not understand yet. We look first, find the cause and tell you how to fix it properly.

The detail

The corner tells you who laid the tile.

A mitred corner is two tile edges cut at 45 degrees and joined, so no plastic trim shows. It takes longer than a strip. It is also the first thing people notice when they look closely at a bathroom.

That is the standard we work to: straight lines, even joints, clean corners and a clean room when we hand it back.
