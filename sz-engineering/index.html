About

A Nicosia company since 1984.

S - Z Engineering Ltd was registered in Nicosia in September 1984. Our office is on Athalassas Avenue in Strovolos, in the Chapo Building, on the 5th floor.

The company is led by its director, Savvas Konstantinou. We work on the electrical and mechanical side of buildings, and on air conditioning.
