03 / About Sevasco

A Nicosia workshop, since 2002.

Sevasco Metalworks Ltd was registered in Nicosia on 22 May 2002. Twenty four years on, the company works from Konstantinoupoleos 6, in the Tseri industrial area.

The director is Kostas Sevastidis, and the company takes its name from his family name, Sevastidis.
