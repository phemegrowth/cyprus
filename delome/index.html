How it works

Four steps, no surprises.

  1. 01

    Send a message

    Use the form or email. Say whether you want to buy, sell, rent or let, and where.

  2. 02

    A first conversation

    We go through budget, timing and what matters to you, so nobody wastes a viewing.

  3. 03

    Terms agreed in writing, then the handover.
