03 / About

A small Nicosia contractor that sees the job through.

A.E.P Construction and Renovation Ltd was set up in 2020 and has held a Contractors Council licence for building works since 2021. The company is run by its director, Michalis Michail, from Geri in Nicosia.

Most of the work is for private owners: a house to build, or a house to renovate from top to bottom. The latest project on the company's Facebook page is a renovation for a young couple, posted in October 2026.
