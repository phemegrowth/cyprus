About

One family, building in Limassol.

34 years
building

P. Pourikkou Constructions Ltd was registered in Limassol in September 1992. It is still a family company. The directors are Panagiotis and Deni Pourikkou.

The office is at 6 Elassonos, Limassol. The company holds a building contractor licence from the Contractors Council, and today it builds houses for clients and develops its own homes under the name Pourikkos Homes.

How a build runs

From the first call to the keys.

  2. 1

    Call or WhatsApp

    Tell us about your plot, or which villa you want to see. We arrange a meeting.

  3. 2

    Plans and a written price

    We go through the drawings with you and the architect, and put the price on paper before work starts.

  4. 3

    We build

    Foundations, frame, walls and roof, then the finishes. You see the house go up.

  5. 4

    Handover

    We walk through the finished house together and you get the keys.
