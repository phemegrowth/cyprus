01 / Services

Every door, specified before it is ordered.

We advise on the most suitable and technically sound hardware for each project, within its budget, and we keep the specification right as the drawings change, up to the final minute.

  1. 01

    Door scheduling

    Every door in the building listed with its hardware set: hinges, locks, closers, handles, exit devices.

  2. 02

    Specifications

    Prepared to comply with local fire and safety law, and to suit the project's own needs.

  3. 03

    Project coordination

    Our project team liaises with the contractor, the sub-contractors, the joiners and the door and partition suppliers until the contract is complete.

  4. 04

    Supply

    Certified products from manufacturers around the world, with German makers for finishes and styles not offered as standard.

04 / Process

How a project runs.

  1. 1

    Drawings in

    You send the plans and the door list, or we build the list from the drawings.

  2. 2

    Schedule and spec

    Hardware set for every door, to fire and safety law, priced against the budget.

  3. 3

    Changes

    Specifications change. We update the schedule with you as the project moves.

  4. 4

    Supply and handover

    Products delivered and coordinated with the joiners and door suppliers on site.
