What we do

Windows and doors in aluminium.

Aluminium does not rot, swell or need painting. Measured for your openings and fitted to close well.

  1. 01

    Windows

    Opening and fixed windows for homes and flats, in the colour that suits the building.

  2. 02

    Doors

    Entrance doors and inside doors with aluminium frames and glass.

  3. 03

    Balcony doors

    Large glazed doors to the balcony or the garden, so the room gets the light.

  4. 04

    Replacing old frames

    Old wooden or tired aluminium frames taken out and replaced.

How it is built

A good window is four parts done right.

  1. A
    The frame

    Square and level in the wall, fixed and sealed all round.

  2. B
    The sash

    The part that opens. It should move easily and close evenly.

  3. C
    The glass

    Chosen for the room: more light, less heat, less noise from the street.

  4. D
    Seals and handle

    What keeps the dust and rain out, and what you touch every day.
