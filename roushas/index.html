About

A family firm run by a builder.

Kostas Roushas has been a building contractor since 1983. He registered C. Roushas Trading and Development Ltd in 1996 and still runs it from Strovolos, with Despo Roushas as company secretary.

He was president of the Cyprus Building Contractors Association from 2010 and of the Federation of Building Contractors Associations of Cyprus from 2012, and represented the trade at FIEC, the European construction federation.

How a job runs

Four steps, in writing.

The same order for a public tender and a private house: look first, price in writing, build with a named person in charge, hand over properly.

  1. 01

    Site visit.

    We see the site or the building before anything is priced.

  2. 02

    Written quotation.

    Scope, programme and price on paper, so there is nothing to argue about later.

  3. 03

    Works on site.

    One person in charge of the site, from the first pour to the last coat.

  4. 04

    Handover.

    Walk through together, sign off, and stay reachable afterwards.
