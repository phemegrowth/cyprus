What we form

Every part of the frame that holds the building up.

Formwork is the mould the concrete takes its shape from. If it is straight, level and tight, the frame comes out true and the trades after us have an easy job.

  1. 01

    Foundations and footings

    Formed to the engineer's drawings, so the building starts square and level.

  2. 02

    Columns and walls

    Plumb, braced and sealed so the concrete stays where it should during the pour.

  3. 03

    Beams and slabs

    Floor by floor, propped and checked before the concrete truck arrives.

  4. 04

    Stairs and difficult details

    Curves, cantilevers and awkward corners. Clients say we suggest solutions where the drawing gets hard.

How a job runs

Four steps from drawing to frame.

For owners building one house and for builders who need a formwork crew they can count on.

Call 99 173322
  1. STEP 01

    Send the drawings

    WhatsApp the structural drawings or tell us where the plot is.

  2. STEP 02

    We look and price

    We check the site and the drawings and give you a price for the formwork.

  3. STEP 03

    Form, check, pour

    Each level is formed and propped, checked with your engineer, then poured.

  4. STEP 04

    Strip and hand over

    The forms come off, the frame is clean and ready for the next trade.
