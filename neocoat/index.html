What we do

One surface at a time.

Every surface needs its own preparation and its own coat. Tell us what you have, inside or out, and we tell you how we would treat it.

  1. 01

    Inside walls and ceilings

    Cracks filled, surfaces sanded and primed, then a finish that looks even in every light.

  2. 02

    Facades and outside walls

    Sun, salt air and winter rain are hard on a Paphos facade. The right coat keeps it clean and sound for longer.

  3. 03

    Floors

    Floors in homes, garages and work spaces, prepared and coated to take daily use.

  4. 04

    Roofs, terraces and balconies

    Flat roofs, terraces and balconies sealed and finished before the winter rain.
