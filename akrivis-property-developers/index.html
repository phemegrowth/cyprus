How it works

Buying a villa, step by step.

One company from the first call to the handover, with one person to speak to.

  1. 01

    Call or send a WhatsApp.

    Tell us what you are looking for: size, budget and when you want to move in.

  2. 02

    See the plot and the plans.

    Visit Oroklini and go through the drawings with us at the office in Larnaca.

  3. 03

    Set your requirements.

    Your villa is built according to what you ask for, so the details are agreed with you, villa by villa.

  4. 04

    Built, then handed over.

    The villa is finished to energy class A and the keys are handed to you.
