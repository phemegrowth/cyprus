03 / History

From the early days of the industry to today.

GCC started when the Cyprus construction industry was in its infancy. It grew with a policy of hiring qualified architects, civil engineers and quantity surveyors, and of owning its own plant and machinery.

Many contracts come from clients who choose to negotiate with GCC on an exclusive basis.

  2. 1947

    General Constructions Company is founded, one of the first construction companies registered in Cyprus.

  3. 1990

    The arched bridge at Ayia Marina, Xyliatos, is completed for the Public Works Department.

  4. 1999 to 2004

    9 Safety Shields for 1st place and 3 commendations from the Safety and Health Council of Cyprus.

  5. 2000

    First ISO 9001 certification for the quality management system, renewed every year since.

  6. 2013

    The A.G. Leventis Art Gallery opens in the heart of Nicosia.

  7. 2016

    Hemodialysis Unit at New Nicosia General Hospital and Wing 3 of the Central Prisons, both design and build.

04 / Quality, health and safety

Safe sites are not an extra.

GCC runs its own Quality, Safety and Environmental Department. Every employee is trained in health and safety on a regular basis, and GCC was a pioneer of site safety committees in Cyprus.

  • Quality management certified to ISO 9001 since 2000
  • Yearly Safety Shield from the Safety and Health Council of Cyprus
  • Own plant and a reserve of modern machinery
  • Overseas: Saudi Cyprian Construction Co. and Iacovou GCC Construction, Oman
