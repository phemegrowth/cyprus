From the Limassol Gardeners welcome note

Through the year

A Limassol garden needs different things each season.

What a garden needs changes with the weather. Here is the work we can do for it, season by season.

Spring, March to May

Planting and new lawns.

The right months to plant beds, hedges and trees, and to start new grass.

Summer, June to September

Water, and enough of it.

Irrigation checked and repaired so the garden gets through the heat.

Autumn, October and November

Clean-up and new ideas.

Clearing summer growth, taking the green waste away and planning changes.

Winter, December to February

Pruning and tree work.

Trees and hedges cut back into shape while growth is slow.

All year, indoors

Plants inside the house.

Interior gardening and decoration for the rooms you live in.
