What a visit can include

Agreed with you on the first visit.

Every garden is different. On the first visit we walk it with you and agree what each visit covers.

  • Lawn mowing and care
  • Weeding of beds and paths
  • Hedge and shrub trimming
  • Planting and replacing plants
  • Clean-up and green waste removal
  • Irrigation checks
