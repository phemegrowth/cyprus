About us

Experienced gardeners, now under one name.

Limassol Gardeners brings together experienced Cypriot gardeners who used to work on their own. We look after gardens in the Limassol region, mostly at private homes.

Who we are

Design and maintenance, done by people who know gardens.

Our gardeners have looked after gardens in Limassol for years. Today they work together as one team, specialised in garden design and maintenance.

Most of our clients are private homes. They stay with us for two reasons: fair prices, and a personal touch in their garden.

We work only in the Limassol region. That keeps us close to every garden we look after.

A note from us

Your garden, in careful hands.

We know about gardens, and we know the calm a well kept garden gives the people who live with it. Our work rests on three things: the quality of the service, honest customer care, and fair, transparent prices.

Chrysanthos Chrysanthou

On behalf of Limassol Gardeners
