What every flat gets

The same standard, building after building.

Their own words on Facebook: "Land and Building Development." On the portals, every Stylianou Court from 13 to 21 is described the same way. These are the things a buyer gets in each flat.

  1. 01

    Energy class A

    Lower running costs in summer and winter. Every building listed on the portals is rated A.

  2. 02

    Covered parking and a storage room

    Each flat comes with its own covered parking space and its own storage room.

  3. 03

    Open plan kitchen and living room

    One bright room for cooking, eating and sitting together.

  4. 04

    Covered veranda

    Shade outside the living room, usable most of the Larnaca year.

  5. 05

    Photovoltaics for the common areas

    A 2 kW system powers the shared parts of the building.

About the company

A Larnaca company, building in Larnaca since 2006.

S&A Stylianou Ltd was registered on 22 March 2006 and has its office on 1st Apriliou, in Larnaca. The company develops the land and builds the homes itself. The director is Antonis Stylianou; Stelios Stylianou is the company secretary.

The buildings are small: a few floors and a handful of flats, each one numbered in the Stylianou Court series, so a buyer can see what came before.
