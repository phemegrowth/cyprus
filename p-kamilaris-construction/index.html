03 How a project runs

Four steps, from plot to keys.

Every plot and every budget is different. This is the usual order of things.

  1. 01

    Plot and brief

    We meet on the land, talk about the rooms you need, the budget and the timing.

  2. 02

    Drawings and permits

    Plans and 3D views until the house is right, then the planning and building permits.

  3. 03

    Construction

    Foundations, frame, walls, roof and finishes, with regular updates from the site.

  4. 04

    Handover

    A final walk through together, then the keys.
