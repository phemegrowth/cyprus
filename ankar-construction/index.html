04 How it runs

Four steps, in this order.

  1. 01

    Write to us

    Send the form or an email with the plot, the house or the idea, and where in Paphos it is.

  2. 02

    We come and look

    A visit to the site, to measure, see the ground and talk it through.

  3. 03

    Price and plan

    A written price and a plan of the stages, so you know what happens and when.

  4. 04

    Build and hand over

    The work runs to the plan, and you get the keys when it is finished.
