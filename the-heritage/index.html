What we do

Four things, done properly.

Agents, valuers and consultants under one roof. You deal with the same office from the first call to the keys.

  1. 01

    Sales

    Apartments, houses and plots in Limassol. For sellers, a realistic asking price, good photos and qualified buyers. For buyers, a short list that fits, not a long one that does not.

    Rentals

About

In Limassol since 1980.

The Heritage opened in 1980 as real estate agents, property valuers and consultants. The office is still at Ioannou Polemi in the centre of Limassol, near the Petrou and Pavlou area where many of its rentals are listed.

Their own words, from the agency profile: it was set up to give people buying, selling or renting in Cyprus "comprehensive and valuable support", and it follows a large volume of Cyprus property records to read where the market is going.

That is the whole offer. A local office, a long memory of the Limassol market, and someone who picks up the phone.

How it works

From the first call to the keys.

  1. 1

    Tell us what you need

    Buying, selling, renting or letting. A short call or a message, and we know where to start.

  2. 2

    See the property, set the price

    For sellers and landlords, a visit and a written view of what the property should fetch. For buyers and tenants, a short list that fits the budget.

    Viewings and offers

    We arrange the viewings, answer the questions and pass on every serious offer with our advice.

  4. 4

    Terms agreed, paperwork in order, keys handed over. We stay reachable afterwards, as we have since 1980.
