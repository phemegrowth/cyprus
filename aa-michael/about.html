About the office

An office that reads the title deed before it talks about the price.

A.A. Michael Property Consultants is a property consultancy in Strovolos, Nicosia. We sell plots, houses, apartments and land, and we advise owners and buyers on what a property is, legally and in planning terms, before anyone commits.

The work is simple to describe. A buyer wants a plot or a home in Nicosia district and does not want a surprise after signing. A seller wants a fair price and a clean transfer. In both cases the answer starts at the Land Registry and the planning office, not with a brochure.

We work mostly in Strovolos, Lakatamia, Engomi, Latsia and Dali, and take on properties elsewhere in Nicosia district when we know the area well enough to advise on it.

How we work

Three rules we keep.

  1. 01

    Paperwork before price

    Title deed, encumbrances and planning zone are checked and explained before a figure is discussed. If something is wrong, you hear it from us first.

  2. 02

    Few properties, well known

    A short list, each entry visited and documented. We would rather say we have nothing for you than show you something that does not fit.

  3. 03

    Plain answers

    Building factor, coverage, shares and memos in ordinary language. You should be able to explain your own purchase to someone else after talking to us.
