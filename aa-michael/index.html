How a purchase runs

Five steps, in this order.

Each step is finished before the next one starts. Nothing is signed before step 2 and step 3 are done.

  1. Step 1

    Search

    You tell us the area, the size and the budget. We shortlist from our own listings and from what we know is quietly for sale.

  2. Step 2

    Title deed and encumbrance check

    We obtain the title deed and a search at the Land Registry: owner, shares, mortgages, memos and any other encumbrance.

  3. Step 3

    Planning zone and building factor

    Zone, building factor, coverage, floors and height from the planning authority, so you know what can be built.

  4. Step 4

    Contract and deposit

    A sale agreement drawn up by your lawyer, a deposit held as agreed, and the contract lodged at the Land Registry for your protection.

  5. Step 5

    Transfer at the Land Registry

    Balance paid, transfer fees settled, and the title deed issued in your name at the District Lands Office.
