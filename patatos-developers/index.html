03 / About

A family name in Nicosia since 1985.

Pantelis Patatos Developers was registered in Nicosia in 2022. It is run by Pantelis Patatos, its director, from the office in Makedonitissa.

The family business behind it, Nianpa (Patatos) Ltd, has supplied heating and mechanical equipment in Nicosia since 1985, working alongside architects, consultants and contractors. The development company builds on that experience.
