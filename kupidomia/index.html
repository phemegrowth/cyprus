What we do

Everything between "I like it" and the keys.

  1. 01

    Property for sale

    New builds at developer prices, resale homes, villas, land and commercial property. New homes come finished: fitted kitchen, wardrobes, doors, floors and bathrooms.

  2. 02

    Viewing trips

    A short trip to see the island and the homes that fit your wish list, and choose on the spot.

  3. 03

    Legal support

    The contract drafted in your interest, translated, registered at the Land Registry, stamp duty paid and the Council of Ministers permit obtained.
