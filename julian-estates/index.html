Plain talk

Always ask to see the title deed before you buy.

In Cyprus the title deed is the registered proof of ownership, issued by the Land Registry. It says who owns the property and what is registered against it.

Some properties in the Paphos district were sold years ago without a deed being issued yet. They can still be bought, but it takes more care and a good lawyer. We tell you which is which before you fall for a place.

  • We ask every seller for the title deed before we list.
  • Your own independent lawyer does the Land Registry search.
  • No deposit until you know exactly what you are buying.
