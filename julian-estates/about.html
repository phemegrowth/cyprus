Why Pegeia and Coral Bay.

The villages west of Paphos have a mix that suits many buyers: Pegeia on the hill with its square and views, Coral Bay with its beach and avenue, Tala, Kissonerga and Sea Caves around them. A lot of our buyers are British or from elsewhere in Europe, some are Cypriots moving within the district.

We only work here. That means we know which complexes have deeds issued, which roads flood in winter and which cafes open all year. It is not a sales trick, it is the reason a local agent exists.

How we work.

  1. Title deeds firstWe ask every seller for the deed before we list, and we tell buyers plainly if one is missing.
  2. Honest viewingsIf the neighbour has a barking dog or the road is unpaved, you hear it from us, not after you sign.
  3. Lawyers, not shortcutsYou choose your own independent lawyer. We work with them, we never replace them.
