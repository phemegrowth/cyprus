03About us

A registered agent that knows Paphos village by village.

“We specialise in a non-pressurised sales approach and show the clients exactly what they want to see within their budget.”

Alexiou Real Estate is a registered and licensed estate agent, Reg. No. 252, with an office on Poseidonos Avenue in Kato Paphos. We advise on every kind of purchase: resale homes, off plan investments, custom built villas and village houses, from Polis and the Akamas to Pissouri.

We are members of the Cyprus Real Estate Agents Association, which only licensed agents can join, of FIABCI Cyprus and of the European Estate Agents Union.
