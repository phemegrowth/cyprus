What we do

The structure comes first.

Every building stands on its structure. The list below is the work we cover, from the first drawings to the finished shell.

  1. 01

    Concrete formwork

    Forms set out, built and struck for foundations, columns, walls and slabs, with props and shoring until the concrete carries itself.

  2. 02

    Concrete frames and slabs

    The reinforced concrete skeleton of a house or a block of flats, floor by floor, poured to the engineer's drawings.

  3. 03

    Building projects

    Homes and buildings taken from an empty plot to a finished shell, ready for the trades that follow.

How a project runs

Four steps, in order.

No two sites are the same, but the order of work is. This is how a job with us usually goes.

  2. 01

    You call or send a WhatsApp.

    Tell us where the plot is, what is being built and when you want to start.

  3. 02

    We read the drawings and see the site.

    The architect's and engineer's drawings set the work; the site tells us how to get it done.

  4. 03

    You get a written quote.

    What is included, what is not, and how long each stage takes.

  5. 04

    We build, and keep you informed.

    Floor by floor, with you kept up to date along the way.
