01 For owners

Everything a landlord has to do, done for you.

Seven things that fill an owner's year. We take them on, one property or several, whether you live in Limassol or abroad.

Ask about your property

  1. Rent is collected each month and passed on to you. When a payment is late, we follow it up, so you do not have to.

  2. Loan instalments, electricity, water and communal charges paid on time, from the rent or from the account you set up for the property.

  3. A clear record of what came in and what went out for each property, ready for you or your accountant.

  4. Finding the tenant, showing the property and putting the tenancy agreement in place.

  5. Deposits held and recorded, then settled properly at the end of the tenancy.

  6. Visits to the property while it is let and between tenants, so you know its condition without having to be here.

  7. Small repairs arranged quickly. Larger work agreed with you before it starts.

05 How it starts

Four steps from first call to handing over the keys.

  1. 1

    A call or a message.

    Tell us about the property, where it is and what you need: management, a tenant, or a sale.

  2. 2

    A visit.

    We see the property and agree together what we will look after.

  3. 3

    A written agreement.

    Duties and fees set out on paper before anything starts.

  4. 4

    We take over.

    Rent, bills, inspections and repairs are handled from here, and you hear from us as things happen.
