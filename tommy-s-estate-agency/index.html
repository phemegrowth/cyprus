How it runs

From the first call to the keys.

The same four steps whether you buy, rent, sell or let.

  1. 01

    You call or message

    Tell us what you want and the budget, or what you own and where it is.

  2. 02

    We match or list

    Buyers and tenants get a short list. Owners get a price talk and a place in the window.

  3. 03

    Viewings

    We arrange the viewings and come along, so questions get answered on the spot.

  4. 04

    Agreement and keys

    The agreement is written up and signed, then the keys change hands.
