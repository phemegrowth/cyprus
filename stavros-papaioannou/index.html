Our story

It started with a furniture craftsman.

Stavros Papaioannou began as a furniture craftsman and was soon running a successful workshop in Limassol. The business grew in making and importing furniture, then he bought land for development and moved into tourism.

  2. 1947

    A furniture workshop on Anexartisias Street, Limassol.

  3. 1976

    Kissonerga Development is formed to build and run a hotel on the Kissonerga coast.

  4. 1977

    Souni Home Investments takes on land in Souni for homes.

  5. 1980

    Cynthiana Beach Hotel opens in April with 78 rooms. Today it has 230 rooms and suites.

  6. 1987

    Pan Flat Proprietors starts in property development and land management in the Paphos villages.
