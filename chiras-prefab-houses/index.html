01 / What we build

One prefab house, four ways to use it.

A prefabricated house is made in parts and put together on your plot. That means less time with a building site on your land. Tell us how you plan to use it and we start from there.

  1. 01

    A home on your own land.

    A full house for everyday living, sized to your plot and to what your permit allows.

  2. 02

    A holiday or weekend house.

    A smaller house for the summer months or for family visits, on land you already own.

  3. 03

    A guest house next to the main house.

    Room for parents, grown children or guests, in the same garden but under its own roof.

  4. 04

    A studio or an office.

    A quiet room to work in, away from the house, with no long build in the garden.
