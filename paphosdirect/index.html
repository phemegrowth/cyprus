02 / How it works

Renting in Paphos, made simple.

We moved here from the UK and went through the search ourselves, back when long term rentals were hard to find. So we keep it simple: we bring tenants and owners together, and we answer the questions nobody else does.

  1. 1

    Tell us what you need.

    Area, bedrooms, budget and the month you want to move. We ask the questions that pin down your exact requirements.

  2. 2

    We match it.

    From our database of villas, apartments and town houses across the district, including homes that are not on the website.

  3. 3

    Meet the owner.

    We introduce you to the owner of the home you choose, and you agree the rental directly with them.

  4. 4

    Settle in.

    A car, broadband, UK TV, a solicitor or an accountant. Ask us, we have done it all ourselves.

03 / About us

A family business that remembers its own move.

“We moved to Paphos from the UK many years ago and went through the difficult process of finding a long term rental.”

That search is why PaphosDirect exists. We are a family run business based in Paphos, with staff from the UK who know Cyprus and the Paphos area well, and who give genuine advice on the villages, the commute and the way renting works here.

We are a tenant finding only business, and we do not store or keep clients' personal data.
