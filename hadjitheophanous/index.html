01 / What we build

Building work for homes around Nicosia.

Tell us what you want to build or change. We come and look at the plot or the house, and we tell you plainly what it takes.

  1. A family house on your own plot, from the foundations up.

  2. The frame, slabs and walls that the rest of the house stands on.

  3. An extra room, a second floor or a new layout for the house you already have.

  4. Older houses put right: walls, floors, roofs and the work behind them.
