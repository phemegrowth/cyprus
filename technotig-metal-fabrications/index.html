05 How a job runs

From drawing to installed.

  1. 1

    Send a drawing or a photo.

    A sketch on paper is enough. Tell us where it goes and the rough size.

  2. 2

    We measure on site.

    Real measurements, the fixing points and the finish you want, before anything is cut.

  3. 3

    Made in the workshop.

    Cut, welded and finished in old Nicosia. Steel gets an epoxy primer and a final paint.

  4. 4

    Installed.

    The people who made it fit it, and leave it clean.
