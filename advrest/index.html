How it works

From listing to keys in four steps.

  1. Step 1

    Create your listing.

    Say what you are selling or letting: the type, the area, the size and the price.

  2. Step 2

    Add photos and details.

    Clear photos and plain details answer the first questions before anyone has to ask.

  3. Step 3

    People search and find it.

    Buyers and tenants filter by what they need, and your property shows up when it fits.

  4. Step 4

    They get in touch.

    You speak with the people who are interested and agree the next step together.
