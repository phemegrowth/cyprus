About

A Limassol family name in property.

Kriel Properties Ltd was registered in Limassol in 1967. Today KRIEL Luxury Living is the sister company of D. Zavos Group, which has worked in the Cyprus property market since 1980.

The company is led by its directors, Elina Zavou and Kristie Zavou. Elina heads property management. Kristie heads the legal department.

A helping hand in the discovery of the property, or properties, of Your dreams.
